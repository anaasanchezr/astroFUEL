Rendezvous and proximity operations are autonomous, with nominal ground/client coordination and a safe RPO state after communication loss.

Keep-out-zone compliance and a nominal hold point at approximately 500 m before continuing the approach.

Autonomous safety

Chapter 7 · Orbital Elements

Raise the orbit. Remove eccentricity. Flatten inclination.

The transfer is simultaneous orbit raising and plane correction. The residual eccentricity at arrival is removed by the first station-keeping manoeuvre.
