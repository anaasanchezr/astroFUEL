1 / INTRODUCTION

Management framework for Mission Lazarus.

The purpose of this Management Plan is to define how the LAZARUS project is organized, planned and controlled during its development.

LAZARUS is the first mission developed by astroFUEL and is focused on demonstrating an in-orbit refuelling service for satellites in geostationary orbit. The mission requires the coordination of several technical areas working together on the spacecraft, the refuelling payload, mission operations and launcher-related activities.

For this reason, a clear management structure is required to define responsibilities, organize the work, establish the main project phases and plan the activities needed throughout the development. This Management Plan provides the common framework used by the different technical areas to coordinate their work and follow the evolution of the project.

The document presents the internal organization of astroFUEL for the LAZARUS mission, the Project Breakdown Structure (PBS), the project phasing, the project planning, the main project risks and the Work Breakdown Structure (WBS). Both the preliminary design stage and the complete project lifecycle are considered, providing a general view of how the mission is expected to progress from its initial definition to operations and End of Life.
